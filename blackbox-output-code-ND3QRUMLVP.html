🧪 Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)

Soil Organic Carbon Detection by Walkley-Black Wet Oxidation Titration Method

Using Potassium Dichromate (K₂Cr₂O₇) and Sulfuric Acid (H₂SO₄)

1. Principle

Organic carbon in soil is oxidized by potassium dichromate (K₂Cr₂O₇) in the presence of concentrated sulfuric acid (H₂SO₄). The excess dichromate is back-titrated with ferrous ammonium sulfate (Fe(NH₄)₂(SO₄)₂·6H₂O) using diphenylamine indicator.

Reaction:
C + 2Cr₂O₇²⁻ + 16H⁺ → 2Cr³⁺ + 2CO₂ + 8H₂O
% SOC = [(Blank - Sample) × N × 0.003 × 100 × 100] / (W × 1.33 × 1000)

2. Reagents Required

Reagent Preparation Storage
0.5N K₂Cr₂O₇ Dissolve 24.52g dried K₂Cr₂O₇ in 1L distilled water Amber bottle, RT
0.5N Ferrous Ammonium Sulfate Dissolve 196g Fe(NH₄)₂(SO₄)₂·6H₂O in 1L water + 20mL H₂SO₄ Dark bottle, standardize daily
Conc. H₂SO₄ (98%) Analytical grade Glass bottle
0.2% Diphenylamine indicator 0.2g in 100mL conc. H₂SO₄ Dark bottle
Ag₂SO₄ (catalyst) - RT

3. Apparatus Required

  • 250mL conical flasks (borosilicate glass)
  • 50mL burette with 0.1mL graduations
  • Analytical balance (0.1mg precision)
  • Mechanical shaker or heating mantle (300°C)
  • Volumetric pipettes (10mL, 20mL)
  • Funnels, filter papers (Whatman No.42)
  • Wash bottles, measuring cylinders

4. Safety Precautions ⚠️

CRITICAL SAFETY WARNINGS:
  • Wear PPE: Safety goggles, nitrile gloves, lab coat, closed shoes
  • Work in fume hood - H₂SO₄ generates toxic fumes
  • Never add water to conc. H₂SO₄ (always acid to water)
  • K₂Cr₂O₇ is carcinogenic - handle with extreme care
  • Neutralize waste with NaOH before disposal
  • Have emergency eyewash/shower accessible

5. Procedure

5.1 Sample Preparation

  1. Air-dry soil samples (<2mm sieve)
  2. Weigh 0.5g soil (W) into 250mL conical flask

5.2 Blank Preparation

  1. Prepare 2 blanks (no soil) following same procedure

5.3 Digestion

  1. Add 10mL 0.5N K₂Cr₂O₇ to flask
  2. Add 0.8g Ag₂SO₄ catalyst
  3. Swirl gently to mix
  4. Add 20mL conc. H₂SO₄ slowly down flask wall
  5. Swirl carefully (exothermic reaction!)
  6. Heat at 300°C for 30 minutes or shake 30 min at RT
  7. Cool to room temperature
  8. Dilute to 200mL with distilled water

5.4 Titration

  1. Add 2-3 drops 0.2% diphenylamine indicator
  2. Titrate with 0.5N ferrous ammonium sulfate from greenish-brown to reddish-brown endpoint
  3. Record volume (V₁ = sample, V₂ = blank)

6. Calculation

% SOC = [(V₂ - V₁) × N × 0.003 × 100 × 100] / (W × 1.33 × 1000)

Where:
V₂ = Blank titer volume (mL)
V₁ = Sample titer volume (mL)
N = Normality of Fe(NH₄)₂(SO₄)₂
W = Weight of soil sample (g)
0.003 = 3mg carbon equivalent to 1mL 0.1N dichromate
1.33 = Correction factor for incomplete oxidation

Example:

Blank = 25.0mL, Sample = 18.5mL, N = 0.5, W = 0.5g

% SOC = [(25.0 - 18.5) × 0.5 × 0.003 × 100 × 100] / (0.5 × 1.33 × 1000) = 2.11%

7. Quality Control

  • Run duplicate samples (RPD < 10%)
  • Include certified reference material
  • Standardize ferrous solution daily
  • Blank should be 24-26mL for 0.5N reagents

8. Troubleshooting

ProblemSolution
High blank valuesClean glassware with dichromate wash
Endpoint driftAdd indicator after cooling completely
Low recoveryIncrease digestion time/temperature
Chloride interferenceAdd more Ag₂SO₄ catalyst
